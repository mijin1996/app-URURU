# Ururu Terms of Service

## Article 1 (Purpose)
These terms set out the rights, duties and rules of use between users and the operator.

## Article 2 (Service)
1. The service lets people who became friends by ID exchange messages, voice messages and stories.
2. Messages and stories are **automatically deleted 24 hours after they are sent.** (Saved messages are an exception.)
3. The service includes finding users and sharing your location.

## Article 3 (Joining)
1. Only people **aged 19 or older** can join.
2. You can change your nickname at any time.
3. You must not join with someone else's information or with a false date of birth.

## Article 4 (Prohibited conduct)
Users must not do any of the following. Violations will restrict your use under Article 6.
1. Profanity, threats, harassment, bullying or hate speech
2. Sending obscene material or sexual messages/photos to people who don't want them, or any sexual conduct toward minors
3. Fraud, requests for money or investment pitches (including romance scams), gambling or illegal product ads, spam
3-1. Messages or ads soliciting or arranging prostitution, or spreading illegal information
4. Pretending to be someone else (fake profiles) or using other people's photos without permission
5. Spreading other people's personal information (contact details, address, location, etc.) without consent
6. Hacking the service or servers, or disrupting normal operation
7. Any other illegal conduct

## Article 5 (Reporting and blocking)
1. **Report**: You can report another user with the report button in a chat.
2. **Block**: You can block another user with the block button at the top of their profile or in a chat.
3. The operator **reviews reports within 24 hours** and takes action under these terms and the chat rules.

## Article 6 (Warnings and suspensions)
1. **Chat rules**: The chat rules are always shown in a chat with someone you're talking to for the first time.
2. Content that breaks the rules is automatically hidden with asterisks (＊) and not delivered, and the sender's **violation count** goes up.
3. Action is taken based on the violation count. The count does not go down after a suspension ends.
   - **1st: Warning** — a warning appears in the sender's chat
   - **2nd: 7-day suspension** — you can't send messages or use the service for 7 days
   - **3rd or more: Permanent ban** — you can no longer use the service
4. Serious violations (spreading illegally filmed content or child sexual exploitation material, arranging prostitution, fraud, threats, etc.) may lead to an **immediate permanent ban**.
5. Violation and suspension records are kept for 1 year after you delete your account (to prevent avoiding sanctions by re-joining).

## Article 7 (Content)
1. Users are responsible for the messages and stories they send.
2. If reported content violates Article 4, the operator suspends it, keeps it for 3 months and then deletes it.

## Article 8 (Deleting your account)
1. You can delete your account at any time from the "Me" tab → Delete account. If you request deletion by contacting us, we'll handle it within 7 days after verification.
2. When you delete your account, your account, profile, photos, messages, stories, likes and friends are **deleted immediately** and can't be restored. Your ID can be used by someone else.
3. Report records and violation/suspension records are kept for 3 months after deletion to protect other users.

## Article 9 (Changes and interruptions)
The operator may change or stop the service, and will announce important changes in the app 7 days in advance.

## Article 10 (Limitation of liability)
1. Users are responsible for any illegal acts they commit within the service.
2. For problems arising from content exchanged between users, the operator is responsible within the scope of receiving reports and taking action.
