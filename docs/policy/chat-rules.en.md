# Chat Rules

Please follow these rules so everyone can chat safely and have fun.

## 1. Prohibited conduct
- Profanity, insults, hate speech, harassment or stalking
- Posting or sharing obscene material, illegally filmed content, or child sexual exploitation material
- Fraud, gambling, illegal trading, unauthorized ads or promotion (spam)
- Disclosing other people's personal information (contact details, address, photos, etc.) without permission
- Impersonating others or spreading false information

## 2. Illegally filmed content
Distributing illegally filmed content will lead to deletion, blocking and other action,
and your use of the Ururu app will be suspended.

## 3. Report and block
For offensive or dangerous messages or users, you can act right away with [Long-press a message > Report]
or [Profile > Block].

## 4. Protect your personal information
Never ask for or share bank account numbers, passwords or verification codes in a chat.
Our team will never ask for your password.

## 5. Sanctions
Violations are handled in this order: warning → temporary suspension → permanent ban.
Serious violations may lead to an immediate permanent ban and a report to law enforcement.
