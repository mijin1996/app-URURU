# Ururu Privacy Policy

Ururu (the "service") values your personal information and follows Korea's Personal Information Protection Act and Act on the Protection and Use of Location Information. This policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and how we delete it.

## 1. What we collect

| When | Information | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Sign-in | Google account ID (uid), email address | Required |
| Sign-up | Username, nickname, date of birth, country | Required |
| Profile | Profile photos (up to 10), interests | Optional |
| Using the service | Friendships, likes, messages and voice messages, stories, block list | When used |
| Reports | Reporter and reported user, reason, description (up to 500 characters), 1 attached photo | When reporting |
| Safety | Violation count, warning and suspension records | When a violation occurs |
| Location sharing | Approximate area (see section 3) | **Only if you turn sharing on** |
| Automatically | Device language, access and error logs | Automatic |

- We do not receive passwords. You sign in **with Google only**.
- We do not collect resident registration numbers, phone numbers, or exact addresses.

## 2. Why we use it

1. Identifying members, handling sign-up and account deletion, confirming you are 19 or older
2. Finding friends, sending messages and stories, showing the friend map
3. Handling reports, checking rule violations and restricting use, preventing people from re-joining to avoid sanctions
4. Answering inquiries, finding and fixing errors

## 3. Location

- Location sharing is used **only if you turn it on yourself** in the "Me" tab. If you don't, we don't use your location at all.
- Your phone's coordinates (latitude and longitude) are used **only to look up the area name** and are never stored.
- What friends see:
  - **All friends**: province or city level (e.g. "Korea, Gyeonggi-do")
  - **Friends with a paid plan**: neighborhood level (e.g. "Gyeonggi-do Pyeongtaek-si Seojeong-dong")
- Your exact address and coordinates are never shown to anyone.
- Your area stays on our server **only while you are using the app**.
  - When you close the app or move it to the background, it is deleted right away.
  - If it can't be deleted right away (for example, your phone turns off), the server **deletes it automatically within 10 minutes**.
- You can turn sharing off at any time, and your area is deleted immediately.

## 4. How long we keep it

| Information | Retention |
|---|---|
| Messages and stories | Deleted automatically **24 hours** after sending (messages you save stay while the chat exists) |
| Shared area | Only while you use the app (deleted when you leave the screen, at most within 10 minutes) |
| Account and profile | Kept for **7 days** after account deletion, then deleted (no longer visible in the service) |
| Member information such as photos, friendships, likes | Reviewed by the operator and permanently deleted from the server **within 1 month** of account deletion |
| Report records, violation and suspension records | Kept for **1 month** after account deletion, then deleted along with member information (to protect other users and prevent evasion) |

- Deleted information is removed from the server and cannot be recovered.
- Information the law requires us to keep is stored separately only for the required period.

## 5. Sharing with others

- We never sell or hand over your personal information to other companies or people.
- The only exception is when investigative authorities request it through legal procedures.

## 6. Service providers

We use the following services to run Ururu.

| Provider | What they do | Where the data is |
|---|---|---|
| Google LLC (Firebase) | Sign-in, data and photo storage, servers | South Korea (Seoul) and Google data centers |
| Kakao Corp. (Kakao Map) | Showing the map, turning a location into an area name | South Korea |

- Only coordinates are sent to Kakao to look up the area name. No name, username, or other information is sent.

## 7. Your rights

1. You can view, correct, and delete your information at any time.
   - Profile, photos, interests: edit in the "Me" tab
   - Location sharing: turn off in the "Me" tab
   - Account deletion: "Me" tab → Delete account
2. If you can't use the app, you can request account deletion by email. We process it within 7 days after confirming your identity.

## 8. Users under 19

Only people **aged 19 or older** can join. If we learn that we hold information from someone under 19, we delete it immediately.

## 9. How we protect it

- Server security rules allow only you and the friends you permit to see your information.
- All communication is encrypted (HTTPS).
- The admin site requires a separate sign-in and is accessible only to the operators who need it.
